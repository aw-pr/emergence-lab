## Reading

**No candidate beat the incumbent.** Geminium storm (0.414) outscores the best
swept set (0.369) and it does so on the term that matters least to grid scale:
temporal flux 0.0316, roughly double every swept set. Its advantage comes from
`radius: 10`, which the sweep held at 8 — a bigger kernel radius gives larger,
longer-lived organisms that keep reorganising. That is a real finding about
where Lenia's interest lives, and it points at `radius` as the axis for a
follow-up rather than at any μ/σ correction.

**Nine of the 24 sets scored exactly 0.000** — the field dies out completely.
They form a clean extinction wedge along the low-σ, high-μ edge: every set at
σ 0.010 with μ ≥ 0.128, every set at σ 0.016 with μ ≥ 0.184, and μ 0.24 at
σ 0.022. A high growth centre demands more local mass than a narrow growth
window can sustain, so the organisms starve. This matches the kernel's own note
that σ ≤ 0.015 risks extinction, extends it (the safe σ depends on μ), and
confirms the harness detects a dead field rather than scoring it as "calm".
