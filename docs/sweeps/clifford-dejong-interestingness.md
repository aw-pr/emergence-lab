Artifacts land under `e2e/artifacts/clifford-dejong-{clifford,dejong,svensson}/`

## Harness setup for a point-map kernel

Same metric stack as the Gray-Scott sweep (`e2e/harness/metrics.ts`), scored on
the density channel at 128×128 with 260 warmup steps. One point-map-specific
adjustment: the app-default rendering knobs saturate a 128² grid — 14 000
points/frame at exposure 0.035 / fade 0.995 push most support cells to a clamped
1.0, which blinds the entropy term. The sweep therefore runs exposure 0.015 /
fade 0.99, where a cell's equilibrium is ≈1.5× its visits-per-frame and the
density histogram stays graded. This only rescales brightness; the ranking is
about the *shape* of the visit distribution, which is coefficient-driven.

## Two passes

1. **Coarse a/b** (per map, c/d at the classic anchor): a,b ∈ [−2.4, 2.4]
   (Clifford), [−2.8, 2.8] (De Jong), [−2.2, 2.2] × d ∈ {−6.56, −2.4, 0.9}
   (Svensson). 81/81/192 candidates.
2. **Fine c/d** at each map's pass-1 winner: Clifford (−1.8, −2.4), De Jong
   (1.4, −2.8), Svensson (−2.2, −2.2).

Point-map symmetry note: these maps have exact coefficient symmetries (e.g.
De Jong (a,b,c,d) → (a,−b,c,−d) mirrors the figure), so winners appear as
identical-scoring ± pairs. Only one of each pair is promoted.

The three old sets all failed the same way: the orbit collapses onto a few
one-dimensional curves, so the frame shows a small, off-centre line drawing
(coverage 0.29/0.24/0.10) instead of a graded density histogram. The analytic
bounds also frame them poorly — the attractor for those coefficients occupies a
corner of its own worst-case box. The promoted sets are dense veiled figures
that fill the frame (coverage 0.73–0.79) with a rich intensity spread
(entropy 0.72–0.78).

Kept as shipped, for reference (same run): Clifford classic 0.571 (canonical
figure — thin by this metric but iconic, same reasoning as Lorenz ρ=28),
Clifford bloom 0.730, De Jong classic 0.716, Svensson drape 0.702 (canonical),
Svensson moth 0.713. No Svensson promotion: the pass-2 winner (c 0.9, d 1.5,
0.743) is close to moth in both score and character, not worth a third slot.

## Honest limitations

- The composite rewards frame-filling density; deliberately sparse calligraphic
  sets score low even when attractive. That is why the canonical classics stay
  regardless of score.
- Scores are comparable **within this run only** (they depend on the sweep-scale
  rendering knobs above, not the app defaults).
