## Harness settings

128×128 grid, channel 0 (State), 120 warmup steps (the seeding transient burns
off by ~80 and settles into travelling waves), flux gap 4, coverage threshold
0.25 — which counts firing and refractory cells and excludes dead ones.

`dyingValue` is held at 0.5 for every swept set. It sets the brightness of the
refractory state and nothing else about the dynamics, but it is an *intensity*,
so moving it under a fixed coverage threshold would change what "covered" counts
rather than changing what the sim does. Two of the three shipped presets set it
away from 0.5 (0.62 and 0.42), so **their reference scores are not strictly
comparable to the swept sets** — only "Classic waves" (dyingValue 0.5) is a
like-for-like comparison. That is stated here rather than papered over.

## Reading

Every score in this run is low — the highest is 0.205 — and that is a fair
description rather than a harness fault. Brian's Brain settles into a sparse
field of travelling wavefronts; at 4–43% coverage the composite's coverageFactor
is doing real work, and the three-state field fills few histogram bins so entropy
is capped low. The ranking within the sim is still informative.

The ranking is also **almost entirely a seedDensity-insensitive birthCount
story**: the top seven sets are the seven birthCount-1 sets, and across them
seedDensity from 0.06 to 0.45 moves the score by 0.012 (0.162–0.174). Brian's
Brain forgets its seed. At the other end, all seven birthCount-4 sets score
exactly 0.000 — too few cells ever meet the birth condition and the field dies.

**"Classic waves" is the lowest-scoring reference (0.071) but has the highest
spatial autocorrelation of anything in the run (0.32).** That inversion is the
metric's known sparse-but-coherent bias, recorded in the Gray-Scott write-up,
running in the opposite direction: the birthCount-1 sets score higher purely by
filling more of the frame (coverage 0.38 vs 0.043) while being *less* organised
(autocorrelation 0.10 vs 0.32) and closer to flickering noise.

## `dyingValue` sweep — 2026-09-06

### Method (defined before measurement)

This appendix isolates the coverage artefact by scoring firing cells alone for
coverage. The per-sim `coverageThreshold` is 0.99, above every swept refractory
value (0.1–0.9) and both non-default reference values (0.42 and 0.62), while a
firing cell remains 1. Coverage therefore cannot jump merely because the
afterglow crosses the old 0.25 threshold. The threshold and metric stack stay
fixed across all sets and references.

The corrected kernel stores `dyingValue` as float32 and compares against that
same stored value. For every tested value strictly between 0 and 1, a firing
cell spends one step refractory and then retires, so the discrete state
evolution is invariant for a fixed seed density; only the rendered refractory
brightness changes. The exact endpoints are excluded because 0 aliases dead
and 1 aliases firing rather than representing a distinct refractory state.

The composite still scores the rendered scalar field. Entropy, autocorrelation
and flux can therefore move with afterglow brightness even when the discrete
dynamics do not; such movement describes presentation, not a more generative
rule. Rankings below will be read only as an appearance-sensitive signal, with
firing-only coverage reported separately.

### Interpretation and proposals

The highest score belongs to birth 0/seed 0.085, not to a richer version of the shipped wave rule. At384² the four Renderer frames show broad changing phase domains covered with fine speckle, rather than the sparse linear firing fronts of the references. It remains active at 2000, but spatial structure is only0.121 on the128² ranking mean. This is a rejected evidence selection: filling more cells and spreading the three intensities raises the composite without establishing a better motif. At1000, all 24 birth 3/4/5 sets have zero firing coverage; seven of eight birth 2 sets and seven of eight birth 1 sets retain firing cells. Those are observations of this deterministic field, not survival probabilities.

The reference images retain moving fragments, diagonals and wave edges through 2000 at 384². Classic's128² coverage falls from0.024536 at 200 to0.002869 at 1000/2000, whereas at 384² it stays around0.024–0.026. Small periodic boards can lose most wave activity and must not decide app-scale quality. Sparse/Storm have a brighter afterglow at 0.9; this is the already-landed September retune, not a new dynamic rule. No seed parameter exists, so inventing three seed labels would not provide robustness evidence.

**Proposed promotions: none.** Keep all three shipped motifs. The retained birth 0 example demonstrates a ranking failure mode; the sparse birth 2 alternatives have not established an app-scale improvement. No claims are made about unvalidated candidates beyond the128² census and raw readings.
