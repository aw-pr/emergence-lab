### Method

- Kernel `lorenz-attractor`, Lorenz family only, 128x128 grid, 5 snapshots at kernel steps 280, 420, 560, 700, 840 (fresh kernel each, frame B 8 steps later), existing `scoreFrames` + `summarizeMetrics`. Ranked readings carry `sampleCount` and `spread.score`. No new score.
- Dynamic comparison holds `stepsPerFrame` 6 and `fade` 0.992 fixed. Schema bounds respected (`sigma` 0..30, `rho` 0..60, `beta` 0..10).
- 64 novel dynamic sets, all retained, including the ones that collapse:
  - beta/rho: `sigma` 10, `beta` {0.6, 1.4, 4.5, 7} x `rho` {8, 16, 22, 26, 50, 54, 57, 60} (32 sets, c01..c32).
  - sigma/rho: `beta` 2.6667, `sigma` {1.5, 4, 20, 28} x `rho` {14, 20, 24, 30, 34, 48, 54, 60} (32 sets, c33..c64).
- 12 presentation refinements (inside the 24-set allowance, c65..c76): the three finalists x `fade` {0.985, 0.997} x `stepsPerFrame` {3, 12}.
- 4 current shipped references (`142-current-*`, copied from `src/app/presets.ts`). Only the historical classic reference is stale (`stepsPerFrame` 6, `fade` 0.992 against shipped 8 / 0.985); the historical wide-wings reference (12 / 0.99) already matches the shipped preset. Current rows are added under new ids for a uniform set; historical rows are untouched.
- Extra diagnostics (not scores, not class labels): a node-side RK4 replica of the kernel reports non-finite fraction, off-frame clipping fraction (trajectory points outside the 128x128 mapping) and lobe switches over the 848-frame horizon; the density frame at step 560 reports the fraction of cells at channel endpoints and the left/right mass split. Regime descriptions below are manual visual findings.
- Determinism: no RNG; start (0.1, 0, 0), fixed-step RK4 `dt` 0.005, metric version `lorenz-unexplored-v1`.

### What the 64 dynamic sets did

- **36 of 64 show no lobe switch over the 848-frame horizon, and 34 of those converge to a fixed point** (end speed <= 0.003 in the retained node-side replica). Zero switches alone does not establish convergence: c10 (`sigma` 10, `beta` 1.4, `rho` 16; end speed 0.27) and c46 (`sigma` 4, `rho` 48; end speed 0.096) are still spiralling in, so they are transients, not fixed points. The 34 converged sets render as a single dot, score 0.002..0.04 and sit at the bottom of the ranking: equilibrium outcomes, not tuning failures. They are the lowest-`rho` sets of each slice (`rho` 8 at `beta` 0.6 and 1.4; `rho` <= 26 at `beta` 4.5; every `beta` 7 set; every `sigma` 1.5 set; `sigma` 4 at `rho` 14..34; `sigma` 20 and 28 at the lowest `rho` values), see the ranked JSON for the exact ids. `sigma` 4 is not equilibrium throughout: c47 (`rho` 54) has 19 lobe switches, 21% off-frame clipping and an end speed of 101, so it is sustained chaos.
- **17 of 64 are sustained chaos but clipped**: more than 10% of trajectory points fall outside the frame (the kernel's fixed x range -30..30 and y range 0..60 is too small for rho >= 50, beta 4.5, or sigma 28 with rho >= 54). The top four dynamic scores are all in this group.
- **Unclipped sustained two-lobe chaos** is a narrower set: `sigma` 20 and 28 at `rho` 30..34 (c52, c53, c61), `beta` 1.4 at `rho` 22..26 (c11, c12; c12 is visibly one-lobe dominated) and `beta` 0.6 at `rho` 22..26 (c03, c04; low scores, 0.28..0.30).
- Transient/near-equilibrium: c46 (`sigma` 4, `rho` 48; end speed 0.096) and c10 (end speed 0.27) are decaying spirals rather than true fixed points.

### Interpretation

- The metric shortlist is dominated by clipped trajectories. The highest-scoring unrefined dynamic set (c23, `beta` 4.5, `rho` 57) loses 28% of its trajectory off the bottom of the frame; the image shows arches running off the canvas. It is kept as the rejected negative control (`142-c23-*` images). The score rewards the extra spread; it does not see the clipping.
- Among unclipped candidates, sigma 20/28 at `rho` 34 gives a balanced two-lobe butterfly, wider and more open than the old `sigma` 8..14 grid produced (c53 reads as two well-filled, evenly lit lobes; c61 is taller and thinner with an initial vertical stroke). Neither looks richer than shipped Dense wings, whose nested loops fill the frame more.
- Presentation refinement helps the best of these: c53 at `stepsPerFrame` 12, `fade` 0.997 (c68) rises from 0.651 to 0.718, still below Dense wings (0.722) and within one snapshot sd of it. Not a clear win.
- Low beta (0.6..1.4) at `rho` 22..26 gives transient/one-lobe-dominant orbits (c12 shows one bright inner spiral and a faint other lobe). These are visibly different from the shipped presets but thin.
- Beta 7, sigma 1.5 and the low-rho sets (rho <= 26 at sigma 10 with beta 4.5; the lowest rho values at sigma 20/28) converge to a fixed point from this kernel's start point. Sigma 4 does so only below rho 54 (c46 is still decaying); c47/c48 at rho 54/60 are sustained chaos, so sigma <= 4 is not a blanket equilibrium. Low beta (0.6..1.4) at rho 22..26 stays sustained (c03, c11, c12).

### Proposed promotions

**No promotion proposed.** No unclipped candidate beats the shipped Dense wings on the metric or, to this reviewer, on the images. c53 (and its c68 presentation) is offered for the operator's look only. Promotion is a separate visual sign-off.

### Caveats

- Metric scores shortlist; they do not decide aesthetic quality. Regime descriptions are manual.
- Grayscale field PNGs (`*-field-256-*`) are scored-field dumps; `*-renderer-256-*` PNGs are real WebGL renderer snapshots of the kernel state at the stated step with paused rendering and default colour. They are not a live app screenshot.
- Worker repeat of all finalists, negative control, best refinement and references matched the retained metrics to 0 (tolerance 1e-9). That is the worker repeat; independent verification is still required.
