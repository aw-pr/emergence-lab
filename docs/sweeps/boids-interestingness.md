## Context: the 2026-07-16 null result this sweep answers

The Gray-Scott write-up recorded the original boids sweep as a null result:
*"the occupancy-field lens is the wrong instrument for a sparse swarm — all
sets score ≈0.02 because binary point-density on a 200² grid carries almost no
spatial-autocorrelation signal."* It named the fix: flocking "interest" lives
in velocity coherence (the kernel's vx/vy channels 2–3), not occupancy
texture. Stage 63 built both instruments that write-up and the Particle Life
one asked for, and this is the boids re-run under them. This is the **first
run of the new instrument: a calibration run. No preset was changed and none
of these numbers justifies a promotion on its own.**

## The instrument

Boids opts into the point-cloud path (`PointCloudConfig` in
`e2e/harness/sims.ts`; point-cloud section of `e2e/harness/metrics.ts`):

- **Smoothed density.** Both frames pass through a toroidal separable Gaussian
  blur, `blurRadius: 6` (sigma 3) — between the separationRadius axis (5–12)
  and the smallest visualRadius (16), so a flock blurs into one graded blob
  without bridging separate flocks. Both frames are rescaled by one shared
  maximum, so temporal flux stays comparable.
- **Smoothed-field coverage threshold 0.25**, set from a measured probe (the
  raw 0.001 dot-occupancy threshold misreads a smoothed field): probed
  flocking regimes read coverage 0.46–0.77, inside the composite's plateau,
  while a fully collapsed flock reads ~0.07.
- **Velocity coherence**: the polarisation order parameter over the rasterised
  vx/vy channels (2–3), reported *beside* the composite, never folded into it.
  1 means one coherent flock; ~0 means directions cancel.
- **obstacleLayout pinned to `"none"`**: the default reef raster paints
  constant density and pseudo-velocity tones into the state channels, which
  would feed a fixed vector bias into every candidate's coherence. This also
  restores the 2026-07-16 conditions, which predate obstacles.

## Mechanics of the run

The full sweep drives each of the 62 kernels three times (density + vx + vy),
which outran the global 180 s Playwright test budget on 2026-08-30 (it died at
roughly 50 of 60 sets). Fixed by **raising the timeout for this one test
in-spec** (`test.setTimeout(900_000)` inside the `sweep boids` test in
`e2e/sweep.spec.ts`) rather than splitting the run; the sweep completed
headless in 4.3 minutes, well inside the ceiling.

## Coverage of the search

Identical parameter grid to 2026-07-16: **60 sets evaluated**
(visualRadius ∈ {16, 24, 32, 44, 56} × separation ∈ {0.1, 0.2, 0.35, 0.55} ×
separationRadius ∈ {5, 8, 12}), **2 references evaluated** (the shipped
Balanced flock and Tight flock presets), **0 sets skipped** — the full 5×4×3
grid fit the budget. Not searched (pinned in baseParams): `boidCount` 2500,
`maxSpeed` 16, `alignment` 0.06, `cohesion` 0.012, `pointSize` 6,
`obstacleLayout` "none". Presets differing only in those parameters are not
distinguished by this sweep and cannot be promoted on its evidence.

## Calibration notes, recorded honestly

- **The blur trades one saturation for another, exactly as it did for
  Particle Life.** Raw occupancy pinned the structure term at ≈0; the smoothed
  field pins it at 0.964–1.000 for all 62 runs — a blurred field is smooth by
  construction, so spatial autocorrelation *still* does not rank boids. What
  changed is that **entropy now reads the flock texture** (0.523–0.889 across
  sets) and **coverage reads concentration** (0.174–0.948), and those two now
  carry the ranking.
- **Velocity coherence actually discriminates.** It spans 0.199–0.996 across
  the swept sets (mean 0.897; 14 of 60 sets below 0.9). The low readings are
  the fragmenting regimes — small visualRadius with strong, wide separation
  (minimum: visualRadius 16, separation 0.55, separationRadius 12 at 0.199) —
  where the swarm splinters into many counter-moving flocks whose vectors
  cancel. Both shipped presets read ≈0.99: they are single coherent flocks,
  and on this axis they are indistinguishable.
- **The composite's low scores are coverage verdicts.** The bottom three sets
  (0.311–0.629) are low-separation gas regimes whose smoothed field covers
  0.90–0.95 of the grid — past the plateau's top edge — and the Tight flock
  reference (0.429) is the opposite failure, a collapsed blob covering 0.072.
  The instrument reads both as extreme concentration/dispersal, which is the
  designed behaviour, but note the aesthetic call is genuinely arguable for a
  tight flock; that is what the calibration framing is for.

## Does the new instrument separate the shipped presets?

**Yes, decisively.** Under the 2026-07-16 raw stack every set — presets
included — scored ≈0.02, a spread of effectively zero. Under the smoothed
instrument the two shipped presets score **0.918 and 0.429 — a spread of
0.489**, and the full swept range spans 0.634 (0.311–0.945). The separation is
real signal, not noise in a constant: Balanced flock sits in the plateau with
high entropy, while Tight flock is read as a collapsed blob (coverage 0.072,
entropy 0.361). The velocity-coherence axis adds what the composite cannot
see: it cleanly separates coherent flocking (≈0.99) from splintered
counter-flows (down to 0.199), which is the dimension the original write-up
said flocking interest actually lives on.

## Promotion

**None.** First run of a new instrument is calibration: record, do not act.
Two further reasons to act slowly here beyond the calibration rule: the
composite's harsh verdict on Tight flock is an aesthetic judgement call, not a
settled fact; and boids has prior form — a stage-15 regime change passed
numerically but stalled on its browser check for a full re-brief cycle, so any
future boids recommendation must go through the visual/browser gate, not
numbers alone.
