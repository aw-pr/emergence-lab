## The finding: everything above toppleThreshold 4 renders an empty field

Grouped by threshold, the 32 swept sets split absolutely:

| toppleThreshold | sets | best score | worst score |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | 8 | 0.516 | 0.416 |
| 5 | 8 | 0.010 | 0.005 |
| 6 | 8 | 0.006 | 0.003 |
| 8 | 8 | 0.004 | 0.001 |

Twenty-four of the thirty-two swept sets — the entire upper half of a slider the
UI offers up to 12 — produce a field with coverage 0.001, entropy 0.00 and zero
flux. That is a blank screen, not a quiet pattern.

The cause is in `step()` in `src/sims/abelian-sandpile/kernel.ts`: a toppling
cell computes `bulk = floor(grains / toppleThreshold)`, removes
`bulk * toppleThreshold` grains, and then adds `bulk` grains to **each of four**
neighbours. Mass is conserved only when `toppleThreshold === 4`, the square
lattice's neighbour count. Above 4 each topple destroys `(threshold − 4) * bulk`
grains, so the pile burns itself away; below 4 it would create mass instead.

This is grid-independent — measured at 256², 384² and 640², "High threshold"
returns coverage 0.001 at all three — so it is not an artefact of the sweep's
scale. The `initialPile` and `grainsPerStep` axes make no difference above
threshold 4 either.

## Promotion

**None — escalated to the operator.** The clean numeric win is available (the
top swept set beats the best incumbent 0.516 vs 0.417, +23.7%), but taking it
would mean rewriting the "High threshold" preset to `toppleThreshold: 4`, which
deletes the only thing that preset is for. Both are recorded:

| | params | score |
|---|---|---|
| incumbent | High threshold: pile 350 000, **thr 8**, grains 8 | 0.004 |
| candidate | pile 25 000, **thr 4**, grains 32 | 0.516 |

The right fix is almost certainly in the kernel, not in `presets.ts`: either
scale the per-neighbour share so a topple is conservative at any threshold, or
cap the slider at 4 and stop offering a setting that blanks the sim. Both are
kernel changes and out of scope for this stage, which is why nothing was
promoted here. Flagged for a follow-up card.

Classic critical and Fast avalanches are both fine (0.416, 0.417, mid-pack among
the threshold-4 sets) and are left alone.
