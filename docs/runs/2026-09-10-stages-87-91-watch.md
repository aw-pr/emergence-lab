### What the design session found

Read against `dev` at `27f10e58`, verified in a worktree cut from it.

**The zoom is a camera dolly and nothing else.** `dolly(factor)` at
`src/app/orbit3d.ts:1282` mutates `this.camera.distance` and no other state,
clamped to `CAMERA_MIN_DISTANCE = 0.35` and `CAMERA_MAX_DISTANCE = 12`
(`:635`, `:637`). Three consequences, each independently enough to make the
feature feel broken:

- **It does not stay put.** `syncCameraToSweep`
  (`src/app/orbit3d.ts:1255-1280`) drives `camera.distance` back toward a
  choreographed value, and the manual-input hold is
  `ORBIT_AUTO_ROTATE_RESUME_SECONDS = 4` (`src/app/renderer.ts:51`,
  `pauseOrbit3dAutoRotate` at `:676`). With the shipped defaults `autoRotate`
  (`src/sims/logistic-mandelbrot/kernel.ts:198`) and `realAxisSweep` (`:213`)
  both true, the user's zoom is reeled back out a few seconds after they stop
  scrolling. This is the best candidate for "it never worked properly".
- **It does not go where you point.** The wheel handler discards `ev.clientX`
  and `ev.clientY` (`src/app/fractalCanvas.ts:270-271`) and the `dolly`
  signature (`:106`) cannot carry them, so zoom always approaches the orbit
  target and an off-centre bulb slides out of frame as you close on it. The 2D
  fractal sims do this correctly — `zoomAroundCursor` at
  `src/app/fractalCanvas.ts:447`, invariance-tested at
  `e2e/smoke.spec.ts:304-311` — and `logistic-mandelbrot` is excluded from that
  path by `FRACTAL_SLUGS` (`src/app/fractalCanvas.ts:11`).
- **It turns the sheet into stipple.** Point size is
  `Math.min(3, Math.max(1.8, width / 650))` (`src/app/orbit3d.ts:3179`) with no
  depth term in `gl_PointSize` (`:209`, `:436`). Magnifying a fixed point count
  with fixed-size splats spreads the samples apart while the dots stay the
  same, and the additive blend at `:3170` darkens the result as it thins.

**Zoom can never add detail, because it is not an input to the build.**
`orbit3dBuildKey` (`src/app/webglRenderer.ts:2750`) carries width, height,
warmup, sample count, refinement, boundary detail, slice mode and model source.
No camera term appears in it, so no camera motion can trigger a rebuild.

**The sampled domain is four compile-time constants.**
`RE_MIN = -2, RE_MAX = 1, IM_MIN = -1, IM_MAX = 1` at
`src/sims/logistic-mandelbrot/model.ts:17-20`, read directly by the CPU
sampler, the GPU sampler (`src/app/orbitSampler.ts:647,651,721-731,821-822`),
the ground plane (`GROUND_DOMAIN`, `src/app/orbit3d.ts:721`, guarded by a throw
at `:3835`) and the baker. Fixed domain means fixed pitch; the camera
magnifies a cloud sampled once at that pitch.

**And a second ceiling nobody had written down: eight samples can only see
period seven.** `estimatePeriod` opens with
`const limit = Math.min(maxPeriod, count - 1)`
(`src/sims/logistic-mandelbrot/model.ts:104`). `maxPeriod` defaults to
`MAX_DETECTABLE_PERIOD = 32` (`:31`) but `count` is the kept-iterate window,
default `DEFAULT_KERNEL_SAMPLES = 8`
(`src/sims/logistic-mandelbrot/kernel.ts:55`). So `limit` is 7, the constant is
dead at the default, and every cell of true period 8 or above returns 0 and is
drawn as chaotic. The period-doubling cascade is exactly what the operator
means by "swirls", so raising the grid alone buys less than it looks like. It
also means `REFINE_PERIOD_THRESHOLD = 8` and `refinePeriodThreshold` in
`src/app/orbitSampler.ts:678` never fire on their period branch at the default;
refinement is driven entirely by `period === 0`.

**What is not the ceiling:** fp32. The GPU sampler's orbit arithmetic is
split-double (`DOUBLE_SINGLE_GLSL`, `src/app/orbitSampler.ts:28-69`, Dekker
split 4097 at `:43`), giving roughly 48 bits, and every sampler target is
`RGBA32F` with `gl.FLOAT` readback. The finest live pitch is three to four
orders of magnitude above fp32 eps. Perturbation or double-emulation is not
what this simulation needs; a variable domain is.

One thing worth recording for a later card: the ELPC bake quantises positions
to u16 over the fixed domain (`scripts/bake-orbit3d.mjs:382-383`), and the
baker's level-2 refinement sub-cell pitch lands at or below that quantisation
step. The deepest slice of a refined bake is currently thrown away by the file
format. Card 87 measures it; nothing in this slate fixes it.

## Token cap to set

**Set `token_cap_total` to 75,000,000 before the first dispatch.**

Size it from `tokens_spent`, not from zero: the window rolls the date at
midnight UTC without reliably zeroing the counter, and it has not zeroed today.
At the time of writing `tokens_spent` is 28,552,112 against a resting
`token_cap_total` of 100,000,000, with `window_started_at: "2026-09-10"`.

The estimate: three code cards at roughly 5-7M worker plus 2-3M verifier, two
document-and-frames cards at roughly 3M plus 1M. That is about 37M for the
slate. One outlier dispatch on top, at the repo's 7.2M worker p95, brings it to
roughly 45M. `28.55M + 45M ≈ 73.5M`, rounded up to 75M.

That is a tightening of the resting cap, not a loosening, and it is deliberate:
it halts the loop with about 25M of window still unspent rather than letting a
runaway card discover the ceiling at 100M, which is how the 2026-08-23 window
ended.

Do not take a drain unless something needs one. The 82-86 run took none and the
resting cap covered it.

## Known harness traps, and where each stands tonight

- **`window_reserve.overnight` doubles as a hard dispatch window.** Setting it
  to express a reserve percentage silently confines all dispatch to that
  window; it stopped the 82-86 run dead at 21:01Z on 2026-09-07. Currently
  **not set**, and it must not be set tonight — the run starts in the evening
  and an 01:00-06:00 block would swallow the first four hours.
- **The top-level reserve is still armed at `percent: 20, action: hold`,**
  which means the tick holds once a reported provider window is 80% spent.
  Claude holds three of five worker seats and four of five verifier seats, so
  Claude's window is the one that will trip first. If the run stalls with a
  quota-gate line in the log and nothing is halted, that is what happened.
- **The token window rolls the date at midnight UTC without zeroing
  `tokens_spent`.** Size the cap from `tokens_spent`, which is what the 75M
  figure above does. Do not re-read the cap as "we have 75M to spend".
- **The vendor freshness stamp refuses dispatch on a stale marker even when the
  vendored files are current.** Cost the 82-86 run twenty minutes. Already
  refreshed tonight; check it anyway, it is one command.
- **A run worktree with no `node_modules` exits 127 on every verify gate** and
  the stage reads as a partial rather than a failure. Every card says so in its
  family notes; if a card comes back with an unexplained 127, that is it.
- **Codex seats cannot open Chromium on this machine.** Every card that needs a
  browser has a Claude seat on the role that needs it, and every Astra card
  tells the worker not to try. If an Astra worker reports a browser failure,
  the card is misrouted, not the machine.
- **Claude seats need `--use-angle=metal --enable-gpu`** or a WebGL2 sim
  crashes the headless browser. Stated in every card's family notes.
